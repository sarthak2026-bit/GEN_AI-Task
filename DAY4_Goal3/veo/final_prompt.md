# Final Annotated Veo Prompt

## The Prompt
> \"A cinematic macro tracking shot of a pristine AuraLux white leather sneaker resting on a warm natural oak wood surface during golden hour. Soft morning sunlight streams through a window, casting gentle shadows. Shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh in the background. Ultra-smooth slow dolly-in movement revealing fine leather texture and precise stitching. 4k resolution, photorealistic, elegant lifestyle commercial.\"

## Phrase-by-Phrase Breakdown & Annotations
1. **'A cinematic macro tracking shot'** -> Sets professional camera framing and close-up perspective.
2. **'of a pristine AuraLux white leather sneaker'** -> Identifies the hero product clearly with brand and material context.
3. **'resting on a warm natural oak wood surface during golden hour'** -> Establishes premium environment and high-end warm color grading.
4. **'Soft morning sunlight streams through a window, casting gentle shadows'** -> Specifies volumetric lighting direction and atmospheric realism.
5. **'Shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh in the background'** -> Creates optical separation between the product and background.
6. **'Ultra-smooth slow dolly-in movement revealing fine leather texture and precise stitching'** -> Directs camera motion and focal point progression.
7. **'4k resolution, photorealistic, elegant lifestyle commercial'** -> Final quality and style modifiers.
