# 5-7 Veo Prompting Lessons for Product Reels

1. **Always Specify Camera Movement explicitly:** Avoid static terms. Use professional terminology like *slow dolly-in*, *macro tracking shot*, or *pan across*.
2. **Define Lighting & Atmosphere:** Generic lighting leads to flat videos. Use terms like *golden hour*, *soft volumetric lighting*, or *diffused studio softbox*.
3. **Control Depth of Field:** Mention *shallow depth of field* and *creamy bokeh* to keep the viewer's eye strictly locked onto the product.
4. **Detail Material Textures:** Describe surface qualities (*fine leather texture*, *brushed matte metal*, *precise stitching*) so the model renders tactile realism.
5. **Structure the Narrative Arc:** Even in 15 seconds, specify an opening anchor and a gradual movement reveal.
6. **Avoid Cluttered Backgrounds:** Keep environments clean and contextual to prevent visual distraction from the hero SKU.
7. **Use Cinematic Quality Modifiers:** Conclude with style tags like *photorealistic*, *commercial grade*, and *4k resolution*.
