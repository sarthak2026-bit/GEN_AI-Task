# Brand Guidelines: AuraLux Lifestyle

## 1. Tone & Mood
- **Keywords:** Premium, aspirational, minimalist, elegant, modern.
- **Prohibited:** Cluttered backgrounds, aggressive neon colors, cheap plastic props, distorted product geometries.

## 2. Color Palette & Backgrounds
- **Allowed Backgrounds:** Neutral studio gradients, warm ambient lighting, natural wooden textures, soft pastel aesthetics.
- **Prohibited Backgrounds:** Industrial junkyards, neon-lit cyberpunk alleys, messy rooms, harsh direct flash photography.

## 3. Product Integrity
- The product must remain the central hero element of the image.
- No competing branding or distorted logos allowed.
