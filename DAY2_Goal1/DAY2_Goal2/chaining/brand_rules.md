# Brand Compliance & Tone Rules

1. **No Unsubstantiated Superlatives:** Strictly avoid words like "best", "cheapest", "#1", "ultimate", "unbeatable", or "guaranteed".
2. **Focus on Tangible Value:** Highlight concrete features and real user benefits rather than vague hype.
3. **Clear Call-to-Action (CTA):** Every ad copy must end with a polite, action-oriented prompt (e.g., "Explore today", "Book a free demo", "Learn more").
4. **Tone of Voice:** Warm, trustworthy, modern, and empathetic—never pushy or fear-mongering.
